Temperament

Bull Terriers are clownish, energetic, and famously stubborn, with a strong prey drive and a headstrong streak that surprises owners expecting the comedic reputation to carry over into easy obedience. They're deeply affectionate with family, often to a goofy, physical degree.

Same-sex dog aggression is documented in the breed, and this isn't a dog that defaults to friendly with unfamiliar dogs.

Health & Lifespan

Bull Terriers typically live 10–14 years. Deafness appears at notably higher rates in predominantly white-coated individuals. Heart and kidney issues are both documented in the breed. Skin allergies are common, and a compulsive tail-chasing behavior has been specifically documented in this breed's veterinary literature.

Nutrition Needs

A quality protein-forward diet with weight management matters given this breed's muscular build and activity needs. Given the allergy tendency, some owners find skin-supportive nutrients or limited-ingredient diets helpful if skin issues develop.

Family Fit

Bull Terriers generally do well with older kids who can handle a rowdy, physical play style. Same-sex dog aggression means caution is warranted in multi-dog households, and strong prey drive puts small pets at real risk.

The Honest Take

The egg-shaped head and class-clown reputation hide a genuinely stubborn, strong-willed dog that needs firm, consistent training from day one. This is a breed people underestimate because it's funny — and that underestimation is exactly how training gaps turn into real problems.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much exercise does a Bull Terrier need?

The Bull Terrier has high physical activity needs and high mental stimulation needs.

What size is a Bull Terrier?

The Bull Terrier is a medium-sized breed.

How much grooming does a Bull Terrier need?

The Bull Terrier has low grooming needs.

Is a Bull Terrier easy to train?

The Bull Terrier is moderately trainable.

Is the Bull Terrier a good choice for first-time owners?

The Bull Terrier is best suited to owners who already have some dog experience.

Is a Bull Terrier apartment-friendly?

The Bull Terrier can live in an apartment, as long as it gets enough daily exercise.

How long does a Bull Terrier live?

The Bull Terrier typically lives 10–14 years.
