Temperament

Standard Poodles are highly intelligent, athletic, and genuinely eager to please, with sensitivity that means they pick up on household mood quickly. They're playful and generally excellent-tempered, but need real mental engagement — boredom shows up as restlessness or anxiety here.

Health & Lifespan

Standard Poodles typically live 10–13 years. Hip dysplasia and bloat are both documented risks given their size and deep chest. Addison's disease appears at a notably higher rate in this breed. Sebaceous adenitis, a skin condition, and some eye issues also appear commonly.

Nutrition Needs

A quality diet with bloat precautions given their size and deep chest is important. Joint support helps given the hip dysplasia risk. Given the breed's elevated Addison's disease rate, it's worth knowing the early signs — lethargy and GI upset — and getting them checked promptly.

Family Fit

Standard Poodles are excellent with kids and generally good with other pets given their sociable nature. They need genuine mental stimulation, not just physical exercise, and the grooming commitment is a serious, ongoing part of ownership.

The Honest Take

This breed's reputation as a fancy show dog obscures a genuinely intelligent, athletic working retriever underneath the haircut. The grooming is real and constant, but so is the trainability and mental engagement this dog needs to thrive — an under-stimulated Poodle is a genuinely unhappy dog, not a low-maintenance one.
