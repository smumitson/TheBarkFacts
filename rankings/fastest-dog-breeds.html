Fastest Dog Breeds, Ranked

Every breed in our Deep Dives, lined up from flat-out fastest to proudly
slowest. A few of these will surprise you. A few really won't.

Speeds are top sprint speed. Numbers marked with a * are our own estimate. Where no reliable published figure exists, mostly the toy and giant breeds, we estimated from size, build, and what the dog was made to do. We marked those so you always know which is which.
